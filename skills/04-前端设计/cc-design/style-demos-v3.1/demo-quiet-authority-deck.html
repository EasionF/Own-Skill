Section 02  —  Product Strategy
MindEcho · Strategic Overview 2026

Three Pillars of MindEcho

01
Adaptive Listening

An affective AI engine that reads voice cadence, lexical markers and on-device physiological signals — adapting conversational tone in real time without clinical overreach. The foundational layer on which every other module is built.

Core Engine v2.4 · 2026
02
Reflective Journaling

A daily structured reflection layer that converts fragmented thoughts into longitudinal patterns — surfacing insights a user would never perceive in a single session, and compounding in value with every entry.

Engagement Layer +38% D7
03
Clinician Bridge

An opt-in channel that prepares anonymised summary briefs for licensed therapists — turning MindEcho from a standalone self-help tool into a measured adjunct of professional care, and unlocking B2B distribution.

Care Network B2B · H2 2026

Section 03  —  Recommendations
MindEcho · Strategic Overview 2026

Recommended Next Steps

01
Validate the clinician-bridge hypothesis

Recruit three therapy practices for a 90-day pilot. Measure therapist time saved per session and patient adherence delta versus a matched control cohort before committing engineering headcount.

Owner: Product · Timeline: Q1 2026
02
Decide the geographic launch sequence

Compare regulatory burden (HIPAA, GDPR, PDPA) against willingness-to-pay and digital-wellness maturity across five candidate markets. Recommend a sequenced three-wave entry rather than a simultaneous launch.

Owner: Strategy · Timeline: Q2 2026
03
Lock the Series B narrative around retention

Position the raise not on total addressable market but on cohort retention curves — the single metric most defensible against the coming wave of AI-feature commoditisation in consumer wellness.

Owner: CEO + Finance · Timeline: Q3 2026
