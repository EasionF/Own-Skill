How MindEcho works

Three practices, one private space.

Each feature is built to be used in under three minutes — small daily inputs, compounding insight over weeks.

01 / Emotion Tracking

Three minutes a day. A pattern by the end of the week.

Each check-in asks two short questions. MindEcho's model maps your responses onto a dimensional emotion space — not a happy/sad emoji scale — so the pattern that emerges is actually yours, not a template.

  • Text or voice check-in, your choice
  • Dimensional model: valence, arousal, dominance
  • Weekly summary delivered Sunday evening

02 / Cognitive Reshaping

CBT-grade exercises, personalized to your patterns.

When MindEcho detects a recurring distortion — catastrophizing, mind-reading, all-or-nothing — it surfaces a short reframing exercise drawn from CBT and ACT research. Everything is generated locally and never leaves your device unencrypted.

03 / Anonymous Vault

End-to-end encrypted. Your secrets stay yours.

The Vault is a place for the things you're not ready to share with anyone — not even your therapist, not yet. Entries are encrypted on-device with a key only you hold. We literally cannot read them.
